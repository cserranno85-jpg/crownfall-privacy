Privacy Policy for Crownfall

Effective Date: May 22, 2026

Crownfall is a mobile strategy board game by Caivra Tech LLC, available on supported mobile platforms including iOS, iPadOS, and Android.

Information We Collect

Crownfall does not require account registration to play.

Crownfall does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information to Caivra Tech LLC, such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, payment information, or precise location.

If the app allows an optional display name or local preferences, that information is stored locally on the user's device unless a future version clearly states otherwise.

Advertising, Purchases, and Rewards

Crownfall does not currently display third-party advertisements.

Crownfall does not currently include real-money purchases, cash rewards, cryptocurrency rewards, NFTs, or transferable digital assets.

Sharing

Crownfall does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

Platform and Device Processing

Some basic technical information may be processed by the app store platform, operating system, device, or app distribution provider as part of installation, security, crash prevention, updates, and platform functionality.

Future Features

If future versions of Crownfall add accounts, online multiplayer, cloud syncing, leaderboards, analytics, purchases, advertisements, or other data-based features, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.
