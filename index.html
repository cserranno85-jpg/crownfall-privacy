Privacy Policy for Crownfall Effective Date: May 2, 2026 Crownfall is a mobile strategy board game inspired by chess. This early testing version of Crownfall does not require account registration and does not intentionally collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, payment information, or precise location data. Crownfall does not currently display third-party advertisements. Crownfall does not currently include real-money purchases, cash rewards, cryptocurrency rewards, NFTs, or transferable digital assets. Crownfall does not currently sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties. Some basic technical information may be processed by Google Play, the Android operating system, or the user’s device as part of app installation, app security, crash prevention, and platform functionality. If future versions of Crownfall add accounts, online multiplayer, cloud syncing, leaderboards, analytics, purchases, advertisements, or other data-based features, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are released.
